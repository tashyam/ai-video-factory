# Title formula

The title asks about a **behaviour everyone recognises** and never names the invention. The video's last line answers it.

| Pattern | Reference / example | Answer in the tag |
|---|---|---|
| **Why [people] started [doing X]** | Why Drivers Started Filming The Road | the dash cam |
| Why [things] have [feature] | Why Shop Doors Beep When You Leave | the security tag |
| Why [place] makes you [do X] | Why Hotels Make You Use A Card | the key card |
| Why you have to [annoying step] | Why You Have To Sign For Parcels | delivery signature |
| Why nobody [does old thing] anymore | Why Nobody Pays Cash For Parking Anymore | the parking meter card / app |

Rules:

- 4 to 7 words, title case, no question mark, no emoji, no clickbait words ("insane", "you won't believe").
- The behaviour in the title must be shown on screen at the reveal (the reference title says "filming the road";
  the reveal shows the camera's view of the road).
- Write the title **first**; the script's reveal and tag have to pay it off.
