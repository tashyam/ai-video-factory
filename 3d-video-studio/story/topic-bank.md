# Topics

## The topic test (all four must be yes)

1. **Everyday:** is the invention something most viewers have seen or used?
2. **Countermeasure:** can it be framed (as comedy, not history) as the answer to a person gaming a system?
3. **One-picture reveal:** can a silent victim simply point at it, and can we show what it recorded or blocked?
4. **Escalates visually:** can the scheme grow at least three times (three targets, a helper, absurd greed)?

Also prefer topics where the scheme can **repeat in full** (beat 1 and beat 10), and where the reveal has its own
"view" to show (a screen, a recording, a printout, a beep, a light).

The origin story is always invented and played for laughs. Don't present it as real history and don't name
real companies or people.
